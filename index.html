A smarter way to use what is in your kitchen

Pantry Pal is a free, browser-based kitchen inventory and grocery planner. Track ingredients in your pantry, fridge, and freezer, review expiration dates, discover recipe ideas, and build a shopping list.

Where does Pantry Pal save my items?

Inventory and shopping lists are stored in this browser's IndexedDB on this device. Your entries do not automatically sync to other devices. Clearing browser data can delete them.

Can I find recipes using what I already have?

Pantry Pal fetches recipe ideas from TheMealDB and compares ingredient names with your inventory. Results are suggestions, not dietary or food-safety advice, and recipe search requires an internet connection.

Does Pantry Pal work offline?

Saved inventory data remains in this browser, but the first page load and some scripts require internet connectivity, and live recipe searches require a network connection. The app is not a guaranteed offline-installed app.

Is Pantry Pal free?

Yes. It is an open-source project with no registration or subscription requirement.
